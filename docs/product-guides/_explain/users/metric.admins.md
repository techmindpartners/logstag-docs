How many members hold the Admin role.

### How it's calculated

- Counts members from the unfiltered organization list whose role is exactly **Admin**. SuperAdmin is a distinct role and is not counted here.
- Independent of the Members table's own Role filter; this KPI always reflects the whole organization.

### Reading it

Admin and SuperAdmin are the only roles that can manage members, view billing and audit logs, and manage integrations — see **Roles & Permissions** for the full breakdown by role.
