# Asset Management: Metric Reference

This page is the source of truth for the in-app **Explain this** panels on the
[Asset Management](https://app.logstag.com/assets) page (`/assets`). Each section is written once
as a content partial under `_explain/assets/` and rendered both here and inside the app's info
panel (`scripts/build-explain.mjs` compiles the registry).

Asset Management requires the **ManageDatabases** permission, held by Admin, SuperAdmin, and DBA
by default. The single-asset detail view is documented separately in the
[Detail reference](./detail.mdx), and the registration wizard in the [New Asset reference](./new.mdx).
